How you earn XP
  • 10 XP for each daily box, plus 20 for a perfect day with every box
  • 10 XP for finishing your pre-trade check
  • 15 XP for locking your accounts, if you don’t trade after
  • 20 XP for each week with a weigh-in
  • 15 XP for hitting your water goal
  • 15 XP for each trade where you followed your plan
  • 10 XP for filling in did well, mistake, and tomorrow’s adjustment
  • 15 XP for each weekly review box
  • 50 XP for every 7 days in a row with 4 or more boxes
  • 150 to 200 XP for each yearly goal checkpoint

XP rewards your process, never your profit or loss, so a red day where you followed your rules still levels you up.
